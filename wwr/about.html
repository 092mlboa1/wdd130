Nile Resort Rafting

At Nile Resort Rafting, our mission is to give every visitor an unforgettable experience on the River Nile. We combine adventure, teamwork, safety, and respect for Uganda's beautiful natural environment to create exciting rafting experiences for both first-time visitors and experienced adventurers.

History

Nile Resort Rafting began in 2019 with a passion for introducing visitors to the beauty and excitement of the River Nile. What started as a small adventure idea grew from a love of the river and the outdoors.

Today, our experienced team welcomes families, friends, and adventure seekers. We work to make each trip safe, exciting, and memorable while helping guests appreciate Uganda's remarkable river landscape.
