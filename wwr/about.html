Nile Resort Rafting

At Nile Resort Rafting, our mission is to give every visitor an unforgettable experience on the River Nile. We combine adventure, teamwork, safety, and respect for Uganda's beautiful natural environment to create exciting rafting experiences for both first-time visitors and experienced adventurers.

History

Nile Resort Rafting was established in 2019 with a passion for introducing people to the beauty and excitement of the River Nile in Uganda. What started as a small adventure idea grew from a love of the river, the outdoors, and the desire to give visitors a memorable way to experience Uganda.

Since our beginning, we have focused on providing enjoyable and responsible rafting experiences. Our team works to make every trip safe, exciting, and welcoming while allowing our guests to appreciate the natural beauty surrounding the Nile.

Today, Nile Resort Rafting welcomes travelers, families, friends, and adventure seekers who are ready to challenge themselves, experience the rapids, and create lasting memories along one of Uganda's most remarkable rivers.
